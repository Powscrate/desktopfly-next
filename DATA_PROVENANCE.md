# Data provenance

## User-provided fly artwork

The app's runtime fly artwork is the user-provided top-view image retained at `assets/sprites/fly/desktopfly-source.png`. The runtime file, `assets/sprites/fly/desktopfly.png`, is the same 56×57 graphic with its dark background converted to per-pixel alpha. A few light pixels inside the original wing region are separated for simple sprite animation; the app draws stylized animated legs and wing silhouettes around the supplied raster body. This is visual artwork, not measured anatomy, gait, or wing kinematics. The side-view reference is retained at `assets/sprites/fly/desktopfly-side-reference.png` but is not used as an animation frame because it depicts a different camera angle.

## MaleCNS v1.0 locomotor subgraph

`data/neural/malecns-locomotor-v1.json` and its companion report are derived from the public **MaleCNS v1.0** connectome of the male *Drosophila melanogaster*. The primary source is the [MaleCNS download page](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/). Credit the MaleCNS collaboration: **FlyEM at HHMI Janelia, University of Cambridge, MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology, and Google Research**. The source and derived subgraph are licensed **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

The bounded subgraph and extraction report were obtained from Denis Shiryaev's public [`desktop-fly` repository](https://github.com/DenisSergeevitch/desktop-fly), commit [`32b00011e83c3dc85fa3ea0b3934155b04f1635d`](https://github.com/DenisSergeevitch/desktop-fly/tree/32b00011e83c3dc85fa3ea0b3934155b04f1635d), released 2026-09-05. The MaleCNS-only data files are separately CC BY 4.0 as described in that repository's `data/DATA_LICENSE.md`; that project's independent FlyWire-derived files, which have a different CC BY-NC 4.0 license, are **not** copied here.

The retained subgraph contains 1,045 neurons, 17,224 edges and 708,689 contacts: 16 descending, 622 selected VNC interneurons, 220 leg motor, 153 leg sensory and 34 ascending neurons. It selects annotated leg motor channels, descending-neuron-reachable partners, bounded sensory populations and real ascending return paths. Each retained directed edge is based on MaleCNS connections with at least five contacts at synapse confidence 0.5. The original source file embeds provenance for the three official MaleCNS inputs, including their canonical URLs, byte sizes and SHA-256 digests. The bundled derived graph's SHA-256 is `8f76d94034dcf802453e3a0a8ed5342d122e57d37e2bb5ea28da66de0856f5d6`.

The measured data is wiring and annotation, not a complete physiological simulation. The app assumes transmitter signs (ACh positive; GABA/glutamate negative; unclear/modulatory zero direct current), contact-count normalization, LIF dynamics, a coarse sensory feedback mapping, DNa02/MDN behavioral command mappings, body mechanics, and a tripod gait phase pattern. These assumptions do not add edges to the connectome, and they are not experimentally validated. Wing animation is not represented by this leg-only subgraph. Details and limits are recorded in [`data/neural/README.md`](data/neural/README.md) and [`NEURAL_MODEL.md`](NEURAL_MODEL.md).

No model or sprite is fetched from the network at runtime. The former NeuroMechFly GLB renderer and associated model assets have been removed at the user's request. `data/configurations/default.json` is an example of local settings.
